# TeX source catalog

`master.tex` at the repository root is the canonical thesis. Files here are
standalone proofs, figures, calculations, and exploratory drafts. They are not
automatically included in the thesis unless `master.tex` explicitly imports
them.

## Figures and examples

- `2coloring.tex`: two-coloring illustration.
- `ComputationalFigure.tex`: computational-results figure.
- `EveningMachineLoad.tex`: machine-load balancing illustration.
- `InstanceFor43Ratio.tex`: instance attaining the 4/3 ratio.
- `LoadComparison.tex`: comparison of machine-load configurations.
- `ScheduleFigureSingleJob.tex`: schedule containing a single-job machine.
- `ScheduleTemplateLarge.tex`: reusable large schedule diagram.

## Bounds and structural arguments

- `GeneralUpperBound.tex`: general upper-bound observations.
- `KnownUpperBound.tex`: known upper-bound derivation.
- `M2UpperBoundNoJobZero.tex`: two-machine bound without a zero job.
- `M2UpperBoundOneJobZero.tex`: two-machine bound with one zero job.
- `MaxOfLambda.tex`: maximization analysis for lambda.
- `NewLowerBoundFamily.tex`: parameterized lower-bound family.
- `PeakIsGood.tex`: peak-structure argument.
- `SingleScheduleMachine.tex`: single-scheduled-job transformation.
- `UnboundedInstance.tex`: unbounded example for the boundary case.
- `UpperBoundInsertion.tex`: insertion-based upper-bound argument.
- `bCutBound.tex`: b-cut bound derivation.
- `lambda_mu_conditions.tex`: feasibility conditions for lambda and mu.
- `m3SpecialCaseBound.tex`: special-case bound for three machines.
- `mKnownUpperBound.tex`: machine-dependent known upper bound.
- `root_free_fraction.tex`: root-free form of the fractional expression.
- `sup_m_KnownUpperBound.tex`: supremum over the machine-dependent bound.
- `unification_lemma.tex`: job-unification lemma and proof.
- `new_upper_bound.tex`: newer upper-bound proof draft.

## Corollaries and research notes
